The Experience

What is a live embroidery experience?

We bring our embroidery studio and set it up wherever you're celebrating — staffed by us, ready for your guests.

A keepsake, made while they watch.

Guests choose an item, pick their initials, name or icon, choose a font style and thread color, and watch it get stitched in just a few minutes. They walk away with something made for them, on the spot. No catalogs, no waiting weeks.

It's the same hand-finished quality you'd find in our shop, just brought to life in front of an audience.
